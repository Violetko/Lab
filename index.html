About Us

The Sports Biomechanics Laboratory at the National Institute of Education is dedicated to advancing understanding of human movement and performance. We apply scientific and engineering principles to enhance sports performance, reduce injury risk, and improve athletic training methods.

Research Areas

  • Motion analysis and 3D kinematics
  • Force measurement and kinetics
  • Performance enhancement studies
  • Injury prevention and rehabilitation biomechanics

Facilities

Our lab is equipped with state-of-the-art high-speed motion capture systems, force platforms, and wearable sensor technologies for applied biomechanics research and athlete testing.
