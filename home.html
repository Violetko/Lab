The NIE Sport Biomechanics Laboratory is dedicated to advancing athletic performance, injury prevention, and physical literacy through cutting-edge motion analysis and technology.

Welcome to Our Lab

Our mission is to apply rigorous scientific methods to understand and optimize human movement and health. We collaborate globally and utilize state-of-the-art technology to drive meaningful discoveries.

Biomechanics

Detailed analysis of human motion using motion capture and force plate systems to prevent injury and enhance athletic performance.

Rehabilitation

Developing novel, data-driven protocols for post-injury recovery and improving mobility in older adults.

Wearable Technology

Integrating wearable sensors and machine learning to collect and interpret real-world movement data outside the lab environment.
